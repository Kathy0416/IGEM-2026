Preliminary Evidence

Data from our proof-of-concept studies in an aged murine model over a 12-week treatment period. Treated groups received our engineered probiotic daily via oral gavage, while controls received a placebo vector. All comparisons are statistically significant (p < 0.01).

Muscle Cross-Section Area

Measured via H&E staining of gastrocnemius sections. IGF-1-mediated PI3K/Akt signaling drives myofibrillar hypertrophy and satellite cell activation.

Grip Strength Improvement

Forelimb grip strength test. Follistatin-mediated myostatin inhibition enhances functional output and neuromuscular junction integrity.

Inflammatory Biomarker Reduction

Serum levels of TNF-α and IL-6. Our probiotic's local anti-inflammatory action attenuates systemic cytokine burden in aged cohorts.
