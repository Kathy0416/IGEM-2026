# Worldshaper-Nanjing Wiki Content Register

This file records the provenance and publication status of scientific claims,
figures, media, contributions, and external assistance. Update it whenever
verified content is added to the wiki.

## Publication states

- **Required** — the team has not supplied the content.
- **Draft** — supplied but not yet checked by the responsible team member.
- **Verified** — checked against the underlying record and approved for publication.
- **Published** — verified and live on the official iGEM wiki.

## Pre-publication checklist

1. Replace every visible team-content placeholder with verified material.
2. Run `yarn audit:strict`.
3. Confirm every scientific claim has a traceable citation or team record.
4. Confirm every media file is hosted on approved iGEM infrastructure.
5. Confirm names, portraits, quotations, and interview material have consent.
6. Confirm the official GitLab repository URL and team ID.
