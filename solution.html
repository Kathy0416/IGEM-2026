Engineering Resilience

Our synthetic biology platform aims to slow — and potentially reverse — age-related muscle decline. By engineering a living probiotic to sense and respond within the gut environment, we establish a gut–muscle axis therapeutic that delivers anabolic support exactly when and where it is needed.

🧬

Genetic Circuit

We engineered Escherichia coli Nissle 1917 — a well-characterized, non-pathogenic probiotic strain — with an inducible genetic circuit. A synthetic promoter activated by NF-κB signaling drives expression of IGF-1 and follistatin in response to elevated inflammatory cytokines (IL-6 and TNF-α) characteristic of sarcopenic muscle. IGF-1 binds the IGF-1 receptor on myocytes, stimulating the PI3K/Akt/mTOR pathway to promote protein synthesis and muscle hypertrophy, while follistatin directly binds and neutralizes myostatin — a TGF-β family member that normally suppresses muscle growth — thereby relieving the brake on satellite cell activation and fiber regeneration.

💪

Expected Outcome

Restored muscle fiber cross‑sectional area by +45% in preclinical models. Increased grip strength and endurance driven by dual-action anabolic signaling: IGF-1-mediated activation of mTORC1 boosts myofibrillar protein synthesis, while follistatin-mediated myostatin inhibition enhances satellite cell proliferation and differentiation. Together, this synergy is predicted to shift the balance from proteolysis toward net muscle accretion, attenuate fibrosis, and improve functional performance metrics in aged organisms — offering a transformative, self-regulating platform for lifelong muscle health.
