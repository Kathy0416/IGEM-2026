The problem · Sarcopenia

Strength doesn't disappear all at once.

It fades through a chain of biological changes—quiet signals that accumulate until movement, resilience, and independence are at risk.

A slow, systemic change

The slow fade of strength

Sarcopenia is the progressive loss of muscle mass and function. Its biology unfolds over decades, while its consequences are often recognized only when ordinary actions become difficult.

30

Peak

Muscle at its strongest

Muscle mass and function are near their natural peak. What follows is gradual, often invisible, and easy to overlook.

50

Shift

The decline accelerates

Reduced protein synthesis and changing inflammatory signals can begin to outpace the body’s ability to rebuild muscle.

70

Impact

Strength becomes independence

Loss of function can make movement harder, increase fall risk, and reshape the routines of everyday life.
